⬇️ Download free on WordPress.org: https://wordpress.org/plugins/easy-form-builder/
🌐 Official website, docs & support: https://whitestudio.team/

Contact forms here, payments there, surveys in yet another plugin? Every extra plugin is one more thing to update, learn and keep secure. Easy Form Builder by WhiteStudio replaces them with a single WordPress form plugin: drag, drop and publish, and your form is live in minutes. No code, no developer.

❓ FAQ
What is Easy Form Builder?
Easy Form Builder is a free WordPress form builder plugin made by WhiteStudio. It lets you create contact, payment, survey, login, registration, file upload and multi-step forms with drag and drop, without writing code.

Is Easy Form Builder free?
Yes. The drag-and-drop builder, multi-step forms, file uploads, email notifications, confirmation codes and CSV export are free.

Can a WordPress form accept payments?
Yes. Easy Form Builder connects your form to Stripe or PayPal for one-time payments and recurring subscriptions.

Can I move my forms from Contact Form 7?
Yes. Easy Form Builder imports Contact Form 7 forms in one click, and your original forms stay unchanged.

How do I install it?
In your WordPress dashboard go to Plugins → Add New, search for "Easy Form Builder", then click Install and Activate.
