# Advanced date field (F06) — no schema change

> [Docs index](../README.md) · Spec: [F06](../feature-gaps/F06-advanced-calendar.fa.md) (§19 K) · Contract: [calendar-engine-contract](../feature-gaps/reference/calendar-engine-contract.en.md) · Date: 2026-11-13

`EMSFB_DB_VERSION` stays **1.8**: no table and no column. Answers of the new field are ordinary rows of
`emsfb_msg_.content`; the field's settings are keys of its row in `form_structer`.

Rules kept:

- `date`, `pdate` and `ardate` fields, their answers and the `persiadatepicker` / `arabicdatepicker`
  add-ons are unchanged. The upgrade to `advdate` (§6.4) is the owner's choice per field and keeps the old
  keys (`upg_from`, `upg_elid`, `upg_value`) so "Back to the old field" restores it.
- New settings live in existing options (`emsfb_setting.cal_admin_display`); the holiday lists are cached
  in transients (`efb_hol_<set>_<file time>`). Nothing to drop on uninstall.
