# Survey Forms for Easy Form Builder — Complete End-User Guide

> [Docs index](../README.md)

Let visitors see how everyone else answered. The **Survey** form type in Easy Form Builder can show a live, aggregated results chart right below the confirmation message the moment a visitor submits — no add-on required, free and Pro alike. This guide is verified directly against the Survey form type's code: creating a survey (template or blank form), the **Form Settings → Advanced → Survey Results Display** setting (Do not show results / bar chart / pie chart), the per-field **"Show this field in public survey results"** toggle and its auto-include-until-first-toggle behavior, what visitors actually see, which field types the results panel can and can't chart, a common-errors table, and a checklist, available in four languages:

- Persian (فارسی) — [آموزش کامل فرم نظرسنجی و نمایش نمودار نتایج در فرم ساز آسان](EFB-Survey-Form-Complete-Guide.fa.md)
- English — [Survey Form Results Chart in Easy Form Builder: The Complete Guide](EFB-Survey-Form-Complete-Guide.en.md)
- Arabic (العربية) — [الدليل الكامل لنموذج الاستطلاع ومخطط النتائج في مُنشيء النماذج السهل](EFB-Survey-Form-Complete-Guide.ar.md)
- German (Deutsch) — [Umfrageergebnisse-Diagramm in Easy Form Builder: Die komplette Anleitung](EFB-Survey-Form-Complete-Guide.de.md)

Each guide covers the same ground in full: the two ways to start a Survey form (a survey-tagged template, or switching an existing form's Form type), exactly where the Survey Results Display setting lives and its three options, how the per-field public-results toggle switches the whole form from "include everything automatically" to "include only what's explicitly on," what the results panel looks like to a visitor immediately after submitting, how results are calculated live and stay anonymous (plus the "Responses" count's real meaning), a field-type compatibility table (choice/scale/NPS/date fields render correctly; Number, Text/Longer Text, and NPS Table Matrix fields do not and can block later fields from displaying), a common-errors table, a checklist, an FAQ section, ready-to-publish `FAQPage` JSON-LD structured data for SEO, and editorial notes for publishing the article on a website.
