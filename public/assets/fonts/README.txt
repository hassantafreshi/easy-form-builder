Fonts for the typed signature of the e-signature field (Easy Form Builder F10-E).

efb-sig-dancing-script-latin.woff2, efb-sig-dancing-script-latin-ext.woff2
  Dancing Script, regular (Latin and Latin Extended subsets), SIL Open Font License 1.1: OFL-dancing-script.txt
  Source: https://github.com/googlefonts/DancingScript (woff2 subsets as published by Fontsource 5.0.12)

efb-sig-aref-ruqaa-arabic.woff2, efb-sig-aref-ruqaa-latin.woff2
  Aref Ruqaa, regular (Arabic and Latin subsets; the Arabic subset covers the Persian letters and digits),
  SIL Open Font License 1.1: OFL-aref-ruqaa.txt
  Source: https://github.com/alif-type/aref-ruqaa (woff2 subsets as published by Fontsource 5.0.12)

They are declared in public/assets/css/layout-efb.css (@font-face "EFB Sig Latin" and "EFB Sig Arabic") and only
downloaded by a browser when a page draws a typed signature.
